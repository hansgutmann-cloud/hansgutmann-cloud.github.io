RESEARCH DIRECTION

Closer to the metal.

The shape of a computation matters. So does the machine that carries it.

I’m interested in the interaction between algorithms and architecture: data movement, precision, scheduling, and the cost of crossing abstraction boundaries.

The cache and scheduler experiments are intentionally small models. Their assumptions are explicit; their traces are reproducible. They are instruments for reasoning, not hardware benchmarks.
